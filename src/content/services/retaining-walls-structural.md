### Walls that hold the hill and look like they belong

North Idaho is hill country. Priest River sits in a valley, Priest Lake climbs into the mountains, and every lakefront lot from Sandpoint to Coeur d'Alene asks a wall to do real work. Vine & Valor Solutions pours **structural concrete retaining walls, foundation walls, ICF walls, tilt-up panels, and load-bearing columns or piers** engineered for the grade and the geology they sit on.

###### Structural concrete we build

- **Poured retaining walls** conventional gravity walls, cantilevered walls, and tiered garden walls with proper drainage, weeps, and backfill.
- **Board-formed concrete** a textured architectural finish that reads like milled timber once the forms come off.
- **ICF (insulated concrete form) walls** continuous insulation inside and out, for tight-envelope custom homes.
- **Tilt-up panels** for shops, agricultural buildings, and commercial structures.
- **Load-bearing columns, piers, and grade beams** sized and reinforced to the engineer's stamp.

###### Engineered, drained, reinforced

A retaining wall that fails is one of the most expensive mistakes on a property. We coordinate with your engineer (or bring one in), install proper subdrain and weep systems, and tie rebar to the spec not the eyeball. The wall stays where you put it.
