### Winters that don't shovel themselves back

A heated driveway isn't a luxury this far north it's infrastructure. Vine & Valor Solutions installs **hydronic and electric heated driveway systems, snow-melt walkways, and cold-weather concrete pours** across the Idaho Panhandle, from Priest Lake down to Hayden and east to Bonners Ferry.

###### Heated concrete and winter work

- **Hydronic heated driveways** PEX tubing embedded in the slab, tied to a glycol boiler loop that melts snow before you're up for coffee.
- **Electric snow-melt systems** cable or mat-based, ideal for walkways, steps, and tight runs where a boiler doesn't pencil.
- **Heated entryways, stairs, and ADA ramps** kept clear all winter without chemical deicers.
- **Cold-weather pours** hot-water mix designs, accelerators, insulated blankets, and enclosures so we can keep pouring when the night drops below freezing.

###### Done once, done right

Retrofitting snow-melt into an existing slab means tearing it out. Doing it during the pour adds a fraction of the cost and lasts the life of the concrete. If you're planning new flatwork for a mountain home, a Priest Lake driveway, or a Sandpoint estate talk to us about embedding heat before the rebar goes in.
