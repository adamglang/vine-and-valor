### The pour is only as good as the pad underneath it

Every concrete failure we've ever been called to fix started below the slab. Vine & Valor Solutions handles the **site preparation, excavation, grading, forming, and soil compaction** that make the rest of the pour possible across the whole North Idaho service area.

###### What site prep covers

- **Excavation and over-dig** for foundations, shops, slabs, and walls.
- **Grading to plan** rough and finish grade, with proper slope for drainage.
- **Structural fill and compaction** imported base, layered in lifts, compacted to spec, and tested when the project calls for it.
- **Form layout** squared, leveled, and braced, with embeds, sleeves, and in-slab rough-ins coordinated with the trades.
- **Drainage, subdrain, and vapor barriers** installed before the first yard of concrete shows up.

###### One crew, start to finish

When the company that prepares the site is the same company that pours the concrete, problems get caught early instead of argued over later. We show up with the machine, the forms, the compactor, and the plan and we hand off a pad that's ready to take a pour on the day we said it would be.
