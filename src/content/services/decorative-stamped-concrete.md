### Stamped, stained, and finished like the lodge up the lake

Decorative concrete is where Vine & Valor gets to show its range. We pour **stamped concrete patios, acid-stained flatwork, dyed and integrally colored slabs, exposed aggregate, broom finishes, polished concrete floors, and custom concrete countertops** for custom homes, cabins, and estates across the Idaho Panhandle.

Done right, decorative concrete looks like stone, slate, brick, or timber you'd pay three times as much to lay by hand and holds up to Priest Lake winters, Sandpoint summers, and everything in between.

###### Decorative finishes we pour

- **Stamped concrete patios, walkways, and pool decks** slate, ashlar, random stone, cobble, wood plank, and custom textures.
- **Acid-staining and dyes** rich mottled color that lives in the slab instead of sitting on top of it.
- **Exposed aggregate** a low-maintenance, high-grip finish that holds up on driveways and walkways.
- **Polished concrete floors** for shops, barns, great rooms, and commercial space.
- **Custom concrete countertops, hearths, and vanities** cast on-site or in the shop to fit.

###### Color, texture, and sealer

We'll hand you samples, not just swatches. Base color, release, antique agent, sealer sheen every choice gets made before the ready-mix backs up. The result is a finish that reads as hand-built, weathers gracefully, and lifts the whole property.
