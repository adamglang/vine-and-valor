### The specialty work that keeps a job moving

Not every concrete job is a driveway or a foundation. The work that keeps a project from stalling **pumping, stamping, waterproofing, reinforcement, demolition and haul-off** is where the wrong contractor costs you weeks. Vine & Valor Solutions handles the specialty work in-house, so you don't have to line up a separate contractor for each one.

###### Specialty services

- **Concrete pumping and placement** line pump and boom truck coordination for hard-to-reach pours, tall walls, and high-volume slabs.
- **Rebar and post-tension cable installation** reinforcement packages for footings, slabs, walls, and elevated decks.
- **Stamping and decorative finishing** custom stamp patterns, textures, and color work for patios, walkways, and entryways.
- **Waterproofing and sealing** foundation waterproofing, below-grade membranes, and penetrating sealers for flatwork against moisture, freeze-thaw, and salt.
- **Bollards, equipment pads, and enclosure pads** HVAC, generator, transformer, and trash enclosure pads poured to spec.
- **Demolition and removal** existing concrete structures broken out, hauled off, and disposed of cleanly.

###### One contractor, start to finish

One phone call, one contract, one crew. From the first demo breaker to the last coat of penetrating sealer, Vine & Valor stands behind every step of the concrete work.
