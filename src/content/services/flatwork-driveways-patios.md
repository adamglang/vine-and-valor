### Driveways, patios, and flatwork that hold their line

Flatwork is the part of the property you walk, drive, and live on every day. Vine & Valor Solutions pours **concrete driveways, sidewalks, patios, garage floors, basement floors, and pool decks** across North Idaho from Priest River and Sandpoint up to Bonners Ferry and down through Coeur d'Alene and Post Falls.

We design each slab around how it'll actually be used: the weight of the trucks, the traffic of the kids, the freeze-thaw of the valley it sits in. Then we form, pour, finish, and seal it so it looks as good as new for years to come.

###### Flatwork we pour

- **Concrete driveways** broom-finish, exposed aggregate, stamped, or stained, with expansion joints and a sealer package tuned for Idaho winters.
- **Walkways and sidewalks** formed to the grade, with textured, slip-aware finishes where you need them.
- **Patios and pool decks** stamped, stained, or smooth-troweled, with drainage laid out before the first form goes in.
- **Garage floors, shop floors, basement floors** flat, hard-troweled, control-jointed, and ready for whatever gets parked or stored on them.

###### Finishes done right

Broom, light broom, swirl, trowel, exposed aggregate, salt finish, rock salt, sand wash each has its place. We'll walk you through which finish fits the site, the shade, and the look you're after. Every finish gets cured, sealed, and sealed again on the anniversary if you want us back.
