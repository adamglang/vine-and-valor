### Fire pits and the concrete that holds them

The details that make an outdoor space feel finished a fire pit that anchors the patio, a spa cast into the hillside, a cold plunge that doesn't leak are almost always concrete. Vine & Valor pours the **fire pits, cold plunges, plunge pools, decorative tile substrates, and landscape concrete** that tie a property together.

###### Outdoor concrete features we build

- **Masonry and concrete fire pits** round, square, board-formed, stone-veneered, or integrated into a stamped patio.
- **Cold plunges and plunge pools** formed, poured, tiled, and sealed for long-term waterproof performance.
- **Tile substrate and setting beds** poured flat and true so the tile-setter isn't fighting the slab.
- **Retaining, seat walls, and planter walls** decorative concrete that does double duty as landscape architecture.

###### Built with the rest of the property in mind

Fire pits and outdoor features only sing when they're laid out with the patio, the sightlines, and the drainage in mind from the start. We design and form the concrete knowing what's going in it and what's going around it so the finished yard reads as one piece, not a collage.
